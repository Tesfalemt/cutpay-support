CutPay

Know who paid, and who didn't.

A ledger for barbers. Log a cut in about five seconds, see what you made today, see exactly who walked out without paying, and keep tomorrow's bookings in the same place.

Common questions

Does CutPay connect to my bank, Zelle or Venmo?
No, and it never will. Zelle has no public app for other apps to plug into, and we don't want access to your money anyway. CutPay is a written record. You tell it what you were paid; it keeps track so you don't have to.
Someone paid me under a name I don't recognise. Now what?
That's the whole reason this app exists. Add the payment under Match with the name exactly as your bank showed it. CutPay suggests which unpaid cut it belongs to, and shows you why it thinks so. Match it once and say yes to remembering the name — every future payment under it finds the right client on its own.
Does it work without signal?
Yes. Everything is stored on your phone and nothing needs the internet. Shops with bad reception are exactly what it was built for.
What happens to my records if I lose my phone?
Be careful here: your records live only on the device. If your phone's backup includes app data — iCloud or a computer on iPhone, your Google Account on Android — they come back with that backup. If it isn't backed up, they're gone. Export a CSV regularly from the Money tab and keep it somewhere safe.
Can I send the numbers to my accountant?
Yes. On the Money tab pick a period and tap Export these cuts as CSV. It opens your share sheet, so you can mail it or drop it in Files. Amounts are written so a spreadsheet adds them up.
Can I book people in ahead of time?
Yes. Tap Book to hold a slot for someone — a day, a time, how long, and what they're in for. The schedule shows what's coming up, and puts any slot that has been and gone without a cut logged at the top so nothing quietly falls off the day.
Does booking someone count as money I've earned?
No, and that's deliberate. A booking is a plan; a cut is something that happened. Nothing appears in your takings until the person is in the chair and you log the cut — which you do from the booking itself, so the price and tip are whatever you actually charged, not what you guessed a week ago. A booking that's cancelled or never shows up costs you nothing on paper.
Can my clients book themselves in?
No. The schedule is yours alone — there's no booking link, no website, and nothing published anywhere. You type in what you agreed with them.
Do reminders text my clients automatically?
No — neither the payment ones nor the booking ones. CutPay opens your own messaging app with the text ready, and you decide whether to send it. Nothing is ever sent on your behalf, on a schedule or otherwise.
What does "money in" mean, versus "collected"?
Collected is how much of this period's cuts has been paid. Money in is what actually arrived during the period, including payments for older cuts. A cut done in June and paid in July counts in June's collected and July's money in. Only money in should match your bank.
How do I delete everything?
Delete the app. All records go with it, and there is no copy anywhere else.
