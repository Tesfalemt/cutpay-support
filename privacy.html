Privacy Policy

CutPay · last updated 25 July 2026

CutPay does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you type stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. CutPay has no backend. We — the developer — never receive your clients' names, their phone numbers, what you charged, who owes you money, or anything else you enter.

Where your data lives

In a single database file inside the app on your device. It is covered by your device's own encryption and passcode. If you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, that backup is governed by Apple's terms, not ours.

Deleting the app deletes the data. There is no copy anywhere else, so it cannot be recovered by us or by anyone.

What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

  • CSV export. When you tap Export, CutPay writes a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes — email, Files, a bookkeeper. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.
  • Payment reminders. CutPay opens your own Messages app with the text pre-filled. You can edit it or discard it. The message is sent from your number by you; no server of ours is involved.
  • Phone calls. Tapping call hands the number to your dialer.

What CutPay does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking of you across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs that receive your data.
  • No connection to Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, or any bank. CutPay is a written record, not a payment app. It never touches money and never sees your accounts. Payment details are typed in by you, by hand.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

Your clients' information

You will be storing other people's names and phone numbers. That information stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. You remain responsible for how you use it — for example, only texting clients who expect to hear from you.

Children

CutPay is a business tool for working barbers. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect meaningful changes unless the app itself gains a feature that requires them.
